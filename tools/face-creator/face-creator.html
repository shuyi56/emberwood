Face Creator

A head for any of the cast (Ranger, Rogue, Warden or your own), sculpted live in your browser from the same formulas as ranger.py: the section-built skull and jaw, the painter's planes of the face, the anatomy-built lids and eyes, painted brows and lashes, the natural lip colour, and the skin's blush, freckles and pores. The defaults are the Ranger's in-game face. Drag to turn the head.
